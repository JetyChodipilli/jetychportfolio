How I Think About Backend Systems

Short notes from systems I've built: the context, the choice, and why that choice made sense.

Workflow Design

Seven roles, one visitor journey: keeping approvals safe without losing the flow

BrainServe Connect has separate responsibilities for Security, Reception, HR, Team Leads, Employees, and leadership. The challenge is not just RBAC; it is making sure each state change is valid before the visitor can move forward.

Context
Multiple roles touch the same visitor lifecycle.
Focus
RBAC + validated state transitions + auditability.
Why it matters
Permissions alone do not protect an invalid workflow.
View BrainServe Connect demo
Architecture Decision

Why I used gRPC between Patient and Billing

Context: Patient and Billing needed frequent synchronous communication.
Choice: gRPC with Protocol Buffers instead of another REST hop.
Reason: Typed contracts and a smaller binary payload fit that service boundary better.
View healthcare architecture
Event-Driven Design

Where Kafka helps — and where it should stay out of the way

Analytics: Kafka consumer groups let analytics ingest events without coupling itself to transactional services.
Notifications: BrainServe Connect uses Kafka for internal calls and workflow notifications.
Boundary: I keep synchronous calls for work that needs an immediate answer and events for work that benefits from decoupling.
